Frequently Asked Questions

  • +-

    Am I eligible for a 0% loan?

    To qualify for 0% financing, you must meet the following criteria
    - Reside in Switzerland and have held a B or C permit for more than two years, or be a Swiss citizen.
    - Be at least 20 years old.

  • +-

    What documents do I need to apply for a 0% loan?

    You will only be asked for your identity document (B or C permit or Swiss identity document).

  • +-

    Does my watch come with a Warranty?

    All new watches are delivered with their original box and certificate as well as the official international warranty. Pre-owned watches are checked by a professional and, in case the original warranty has expired, Swiss Watches offers you a 12-month Warranty.

  • +-

    How long does it take to process my financing request?

    Your request is processed instantaneously from Monday to Friday between 8:00 am and 6:00 pm, and outside these hours within 12 hours.

  • +-

    Can I return my order?

    Your order can be cancelled or exchanged within 10 days from its delivery date.

    Only watches that have not been worn, in perfect condition and in their original packaging can be returned or exchanged.
