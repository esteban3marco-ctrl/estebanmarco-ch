What is the difference between SEO and GEO?

SEO moves your website up on Google and in Google Maps. GEO, Generative Engine Optimization, makes AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google's AI Overviews know, understand and name your business. Both start from the same basics: clear content, clean technology and details that are the same everywhere.

How I work

First an audit: where you stand today, which searches you are missing and what the businesses ahead of you do differently. Then work on the website, the Google Business Profile and listings such as local.ch and search.ch. Every month I measure rankings and enquiries and show you what is moving.

Local SEO in Zurich and on the Gold Coast

Anyone searching for an accountant in Zurich or a physiotherapist in Küsnacht sees the map first. What counts there is a complete business profile, real reviews and the same details on every platform. I build that up with you.

Visible in ChatGPT and AI answers

AI assistants name businesses whose website they can read and that other sources describe the same way. I write content that answers questions directly, add structured data and check regularly whether and how the assistants recommend you.

Search engine optimisation: what I actually do

Technology: loading speed, indexing in Google Search Console, one address per language, structured data. Content: one page per service, titles and texts with the terms your customers really type, measured with real search volumes. Local: Google Business Profile, local.ch, search.ch and reviews. And every month a short report on what has moved.

SEO agency or one person?

At an SEO agency several people often work on your account, and you talk to account management. With me, the person you write to does the work. I also build websites, so I can fix technical problems directly instead of just listing them in a report.
