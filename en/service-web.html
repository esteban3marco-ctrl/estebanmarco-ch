Getting a website built in Zurich: how it works

You write to me about what your website should do. I suggest the structure, pages and languages and give you a fixed price. Then I design it, we settle the texts, and I build the site by hand. Once you approve it, it goes live, and you edit texts, images and opening hours yourself in your own panel.

What a website needs to do today

It has to load fast on a phone, be found on Google and be readable by AI assistants such as ChatGPT. That is why every language gets its own address, every page clear headings and structured data, and the content sits directly in the HTML instead of behind scripts.

No site builder, no WordPress plugins

I build every website by hand. That makes it faster and more secure, and there are no plugins asking for an update or a licence every month. Your editing panel is made for exactly your content.

What a website costs

The price depends on the number of pages and languages and on whether you need a shop or your own panel. You get a fixed price in writing before we start. There is no monthly licence, only hosting and maintenance if you want them.

Web agency or web designer?

A web agency splits your project between a project manager, a designer and a developer. With me you talk to the person who designs and builds your website. That means shorter paths, no hand-overs and one contact from the first email until after launch. For many SMEs in Zurich, Zug or Winterthur that is faster than a big agency, and you don't pay for project management.

Getting an online shop built

For products or vouchers I build the shop straight into your website: payment with TWINT, card, Apple Pay and Google Pay via Stripe, automatic order and shipping confirmations and every order in your panel. No shop platform that charges for each feature every month.
