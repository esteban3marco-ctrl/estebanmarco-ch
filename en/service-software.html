When custom software pays off

When standard programs only almost fit, when data sits in three spreadsheets or when your team spends hours every week retyping. Then a tool that maps exactly your steps is often cheaper than one more subscription.

A CRM that speaks your language

Clients, enquiries, quotes, deadlines and invoices in one place, with your terms and your processes. Connected to Bexio, Abacus, Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace.

You stay independent

Fixed price per phase; source code and documentation belong to you. You can switch at any time or keep developing in-house.

A web app instead of the App Store

Most tools for SMEs don't need an app in the App Store. A web app runs in the browser on computer, tablet and phone, can be added to the home screen and is updated in one place. That saves development and review costs.

Examples from practice

A sales panel with stock, consignment and monthly statements for a boutique. A work report for a trades business. A CRM with a lead radar for my own prospecting. And contracts that clients sign online with their finger, with a PDF and certificate.
