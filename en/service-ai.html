What is a local AI?

A language model like ChatGPT that does not run in someone else's cloud but on a computer in your office or in a Swiss data centre. Your documents never leave the building, and nobody trains on your data.

What businesses use it for

Asking questions about contracts, manuals and client files, drafting texts, summarising emails and sharing knowledge within the team. Every answer names the document it comes from.

What you need

A capable computer that I choose with you, or a server in Switzerland. I install the model, connect it to your documents and train your team.

Local AI and data protection (FADP)

The model and the documents stay with you. Nothing is transferred to providers abroad, and there is no question of whether your data is used for training. For accountants, law firms, practices and property companies that is often the reason to use AI at all.

The approach

I install an open language model on your own hardware and connect it to your documents: questions in plain language, answers with sources, fully offline if you want.

Once it's set up, the system is yours: no per-user subscription, no data transfer, no provider changing the rules.
